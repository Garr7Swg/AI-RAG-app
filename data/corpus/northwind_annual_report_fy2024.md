# Northwind Industrial Group, Inc.
## Annual Report — Fiscal Year 2024 (Form 10-K excerpt)

Northwind Industrial Group, Inc. ("Northwind", "the Company", NYSE: **NWIG**) designs and manufactures flow control equipment, thermal management systems and industrial digital services for process, power and semiconductor customers. The Company was founded in 1974, is headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, completed its initial public offering in 1998, and employed **12,480** people at December 31, 2024 (11,910 at December 31, 2023).

### 1. Financial Highlights

Consolidated revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $4,812 million, an increase of 7.4% from $4,480 million in fiscal year 2023. Adjusted EBITDA was $842 million, equal to 17.5% of revenue. Net income was $388 million and diluted earnings per share were $2.41. Gross margin expanded 150 basis points to 34.6%, driven by price realization and a richer mix in Digital & Services. Research and development expense was $214 million, or 4.4% of revenue. Capital expenditures were $296 million and free cash flow was $316 million. The effective tax rate for fiscal year 2024 was 21.8%.

### 2. Segment Performance

Northwind reports three operating segments: Flow Control, Thermal Systems, and Digital & Services.

| Segment | FY2024 revenue | Adjusted EBITDA margin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flow Control | $1,940 million | 21.4% |
| Thermal Systems | $1,620 million | 15.2% |
| Digital & Services | $1,252 million | 24.8% |

Flow Control was the largest segment by revenue at $1,940 million, supported by municipal water projects in the Americas and Middle East. Thermal Systems revenue of $1,620 million was flat year over year as data-center demand offset weaker commercial HVAC orders. Digital & Services grew 18.6% to $1,252 million and carried the highest segment margin at 24.8%. Total company backlog was $2,310 million at year end, and the book-to-bill ratio was 1.08. The Company recorded restructuring charges of $27 million, primarily severance in Thermal Systems. Revenue by geography was 54% Americas, 29% EMEA and 17% APAC.

### 3. Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $486 million at December 31, 2024. Total debt was $1,846 million, resulting in net debt of $1,360 million and net leverage of 1.61x adjusted EBITDA. The Company's $1,250 million revolving credit facility matures on June 30, 2031, and its $600 million term loan B matures on March 15, 2029. Inventory was $742 million, days sales outstanding was 63 days and days inventory outstanding was 96 days. Operating lease liabilities were $180 million. Goodwill of $2,140 million is tested annually for impairment; the Digital & Services reporting unit had 22% headroom over its carrying value at the 2024 test date.

The Company pays an annual dividend of $0.92 per share, representing a payout ratio of 38%. Fiscal 2024 marked the tenth consecutive annual dividend increase. The share repurchase authorization has $75 million remaining out of $150 million. Management intends to prioritize debt reduction and bolt-on acquisitions over accelerated buybacks in fiscal 2025.

### 4. Guidance

For fiscal year 2025, management expects revenue of $5,050 million to $5,200 million, an adjusted EBITDA margin of 17.8% to 18.2%, an effective tax rate of 23.5%, and capital expenditures of approximately 4.5% of revenue. Guidance assumes no material change in tariff policy and a neutral foreign-exchange environment.

### 5. Risk Factors

**Supplier concentration.** Two suppliers of precision castings accounted for 31% of direct material spend in fiscal 2024, and the impeller used in the CX-900 chiller is single-sourced. A sustained interruption at either supplier could delay shipments.

**Tariffs and trade policy.** Section 301 tariffs on imported motors increased cost of goods sold by $18 million in fiscal 2024. The Company is qualifying alternative motor suppliers in Vietnam and expects to recover approximately half of that cost through pricing in fiscal 2025.

**Cybersecurity.** Northwind maintains a security program aligned to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. The Company experienced no material cybersecurity incidents in fiscal 2024. A security incident affecting operational technology at a manufacturing site remains a principal risk.

**Climate and energy transition.** Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions declined 12% year over year. Future carbon pricing could increase electricity costs at foundry operations.

**Customer concentration.** The largest customer represented 6% of consolidated revenue in fiscal 2024.

### 6. Independent Auditor's Report

The consolidated financial statements were audited by **Harlow & Vance LLP**. The audit opinion was unqualified: the financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of the Company in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States. The auditor reported no material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting. Two critical audit matters were communicated: revenue recognition on long-term construction contracts, and the goodwill impairment assessment for the Digital & Services reporting unit.

### 7. Governance

Dana Whitfield serves as President and Chief Executive Officer. Marcus Ohara serves as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer. The board consists of nine directors, seven of whom are independent; Priya Raman serves as independent Chair. Executive compensation is disclosed in the proxy statement and is not part of this excerpt.
