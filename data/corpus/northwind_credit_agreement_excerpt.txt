NORTHWIND INDUSTRIAL GROUP, INC.
CREDIT AGREEMENT - EXCERPT
Dated as of June 30, 2024
Among Northwind Industrial Group, Inc., as Borrower, the Lenders party hereto,
and Meridian Trust Bank, N.A., as Administrative Agent.

ARTICLE I - DEFINITIONS

"Consolidated EBITDA" means, for any period, consolidated net income plus consolidated interest
expense, taxes, depreciation and amortization, and non-recurring restructuring charges, determined
in accordance with GAAP.

"Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio" means, as of any date of determination, the ratio of
(a) Consolidated Funded Indebtedness minus Unrestricted Cash to (b) Consolidated EBITDA for the
trailing four fiscal quarters.

"Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio" means, for any period, the ratio of Consolidated EBITDA to
Consolidated Interest Expense for such period.

ARTICLE II - THE CREDITS

Section 2.1 Revolving Credit Facility. Subject to the terms hereof, the Lenders severally agree to
make revolving loans to the Borrower in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed
$1,250,000,000 at any time outstanding. The Revolving Credit Facility matures on June 30, 2031.

Section 2.2 Term Loan B. The Lenders have made a term loan to the Borrower in the original
principal amount of $600,000,000. The Term Loan B matures on March 15, 2029 and amortizes at 1.0%
of original principal per annum.

Section 2.7 Applicable Margin and Fees. Loans bear interest at Adjusted Term SOFR plus 1.25% per
annum at the Company's current pricing level. The applicable margin grid ranges from 1.00% to 1.75%
per annum and is determined quarterly from the Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio. A commitment fee of
0.20% per annum accrues on the average daily undrawn portion of the Revolving Credit Facility.

Section 2.9 Voluntary Prepayment. The Borrower may prepay the Term Loan B at any time without
premium or penalty, subject to three business days' prior notice.

ARTICLE VII - NEGATIVE COVENANTS

Section 7.1 Maximum Leverage. The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio, as
of the end of any fiscal quarter, to exceed 3.50 to 1.00.

Section 7.2 Minimum Interest Coverage. The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated Interest
Coverage Ratio for any period of four consecutive fiscal quarters to be less than 2.75 to 1.00.

Section 7.3 Restricted Payments. The Borrower shall not make restricted payments in any fiscal year
in an aggregate amount exceeding $250,000,000 plus 50% of Excess Cash Flow for the immediately
preceding fiscal year, so long as no Default exists and the Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio is not
greater than 3.00 to 1.00 on a pro forma basis.

Section 7.4 Indebtedness. The Borrower may incur additional unsecured indebtedness up to an
aggregate basket of $400,000,000 for permitted acquisitions, plus a general basket of $150,000,000.

Section 7.5 Asset Sales. The Borrower shall not consummate asset sales in any fiscal year in excess
of $300,000,000 in aggregate consideration without Lender consent, other than sales of inventory in
the ordinary course of business.

Section 7.6 Minimum Liquidity. The Borrower shall maintain at all times unrestricted cash plus
revolving commitments available for drawing of not less than $150,000,000.

Section 7.7 Negative Pledge. The Borrower shall not create liens on any property or assets other
than permitted liens, including purchase-money liens and liens existing on the Closing Date.

Section 7.8 Springing Lien Requirement. If the Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio exceeds 2.75 to
1.00, the Borrower shall, within 45 days, grant first-priority liens on substantially all assets to
the Administrative Agent for the ratable benefit of the Lenders.

ARTICLE VIII - EVENTS OF DEFAULT

Section 8.1 Payment Default. Failure to pay any principal of any loan when due, or failure to pay
interest, fees or other amounts within five (5) business days after the due date.

Section 8.2 Reporting Default. Failure to deliver financial statements or a compliance certificate
when required, if such failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from the
Administrative Agent.

Section 8.3 Cross-Default. Any other indebtedness of the Borrower or its subsidiaries in an
aggregate principal amount exceeding $50,000,000 becomes due and payable prior to its stated
maturity as a result of a default.

Section 8.4 Change of Control. Upon a change of control, each Lender may require the Borrower to
repurchase its loans at 101% of the principal amount plus accrued interest.

Section 8.5 Insolvency. The Borrower or any material subsidiary commences a voluntary proceeding
under any bankruptcy or insolvency law.

ARTICLE IX - GUARANTORS

Each subsidiary that accounts for 10% or more of Consolidated EBITDA shall execute a guarantee and
become a Guarantor within 60 days after becoming a material subsidiary.
